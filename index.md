# LuckySips Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** April 27, 2026

LuckySips is a party game app intended for users who are of legal drinking age in their region.

By continuing in the app, all players confirm that they are of legal drinking age.

## Data We Collect

LuckySips may collect limited app usage and analytics data to help improve the app, understand game activity, measure ad performance, and debug issues.

This may include:

- Session identifier
- Device or app identifier
- App launch times
- Player names entered for gameplay
- Number of players in a session
- Games started
- Games completed
- Games abandoned
- Ad impressions
- Ad clicks
- Ad view time
- Basic ad performance statistics

Player names are used for gameplay and may be linked to the current app session for analytics. Player names are not used to create accounts or log in.

## Data We Do Not Collect

LuckySips does not ask for birth dates, ID documents, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, payment information, or account login details.

## How We Use Data

We use limited app usage and analytics data to:

- Improve LuckySips
- Understand which games and features are used
- Measure basic ad performance
- Debug issues and improve app stability

## Ads

LuckySips may show ads or ad placement areas. Ads are not required to unlock game content.

The app does not require users to watch ads to access games.

## User Responsibility

Players are never required to follow any prompt in the app. Each player is responsible for how they choose to play.
