# STRÆX Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 17, 2026

STRÆX is a family-friendly party game app made for group gameplay, challenges, and social fun

Players are never required to follow any prompt in the app. Each player is responsible for how they choose to play, and players should skip any prompt they do not want to do

## Data We Collect

STRÆX may collect limited app usage and analytics data to help improve the app, understand game activity, measure ad performance, and debug issues

This may include:

- Session identifier
- Device or app identifier
- App launch times
- Player names entered for gameplay
- Number of players in a session
- Games started
- Games completed
- Games abandoned
- Ad impressions
- Ad clicks
- Ad view time
- Basic ad performance statistics

Player names are used for gameplay and may be linked to the current app session for analytics. Player names are not used to create accounts or log in

## Data We Do Not Collect

STRÆX does not ask for birth dates, ID documents, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, payment information, or account login details

## How We Use Data

We use limited app usage and analytics data to:

- Improve STRÆX
- Understand which games and features are used
- Measure basic ad performance
- Debug issues and improve app stability

## Ads

STRÆX may show ads or ad placement areas. Ads are not required to unlock game content

The app does not require users to watch ads to access games

## User Responsibility

STRÆX is designed for fun, family-friendly party gameplay

Players are never required to follow any prompt in the app. Players should use their own judgment, respect others, and skip any prompt that feels uncomfortable, unsafe, or inappropriate for their group
